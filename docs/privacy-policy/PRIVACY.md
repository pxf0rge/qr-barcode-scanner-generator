# Privacy Policy — VectorQR — QR & Barcodes

**App:** VectorQR — QR & Barcodes  
**Package:** `com.smartapps.vectorqr`  
**Effective date:** September 21, 2026  
**Last updated:** September 21, 2026

## Summary
VectorQR does **not** create user accounts and does **not** collect, sell, or upload your personal information to our own servers. Camera, photos, contacts, and scan history stay on your device (unless you choose to share or save them yourself). The app shows ads via **Google AdMob**, which may process device-level advertising data under Google's policies.

## 1. Who we are
This Privacy Policy applies to the Android application **VectorQR — QR & Barcodes** ("VectorQR", "the App"), package name `com.smartapps.vectorqr`, published by SmartApps ("we", "us", or "developer").

Contact: privacy.smartapps.vectorqr@gmail.com

## 2. Information we (the developer) collect
**We do not collect personal data on our servers.** VectorQR has no developer backend for analytics profiles, account registration, or cloud backups of your content.

We do not:
- Require sign-in or collect name, email, phone number, or payment details
- Upload scanned QR/barcode contents to our servers
- Upload camera frames, gallery images, contacts, or exported files to our servers
- Sell or rent user data

Local preferences (theme, beep settings, history) may be stored **only on your device**. Uninstalling the App removes this local data.

## 3. Permissions and how they are used

### 3.1 Camera
Used solely to scan QR codes and barcodes in real time. Camera imagery is processed on-device and is **not recorded or uploaded** by VectorQR to our servers.

### 3.2 Photos / media / storage
May be used to:
- Scan a QR/barcode from an image you pick from the gallery
- Save generated codes to your gallery when you choose Save
- Share exported files through the system share sheet when you choose Share

We do not upload those files to our servers.

### 3.3 Contacts (optional)
Requested only to help import contact fields into a QR (vCard) that you generate. Processed on-device; not uploaded to our servers.

### 3.4 Internet / network state
Used to load ads (Google AdMob), show consent forms where required (Google UMP), and open links you tap.

## 4. Advertising (Google AdMob)
VectorQR uses Google AdMob for:
- Banner ads
- Interstitial ads
- Rewarded ads

Google and its partners may collect or process device identifiers (such as advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device/app info, and interaction data, subject to your consent choices and applicable law.

Learn more:
- https://policies.google.com/privacy
- https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543
- https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

You can reset/limit your advertising ID in Android settings and manage privacy options in the App where available.

## 5. Children
VectorQR is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 6. Data security
Because we do not operate a user-data backend for VectorQR content, the primary security boundary is your device. Use Android permission controls to revoke Camera, Photos, or Contacts access at any time.

## 7. Data retention
We retain no personal user profiles on developer servers. On-device history and settings remain until you clear them or uninstall. Advertising data retention is governed by Google AdMob / Google policies.

## 8. Your choices
- Deny or revoke permissions in system settings
- Clear local history in the App (where available)
- Use Android advertising / privacy controls and in-app privacy options
- Uninstall the App to remove local VectorQR data

## 9. International users
If you are in the EEA/UK/Switzerland or other regions with consent requirements for ads, Google UMP may present a consent form before personalized ads are requested.

## 10. Changes
We may update this Privacy Policy. The "Last updated" date will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.
