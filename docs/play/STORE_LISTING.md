# Play Store listing draft (V1)

Use this copy for Play Console. Do **not** claim Editable SVG export as available in V1.

## Short description (≤80 chars)

Generate & scan QR codes and barcodes. Customize, export, share.

## Full description

VectorQR helps you create and scan QR codes and barcodes on your Android device.

**Create**
• Text, URL, Wi‑Fi, Contact (vCard)
• WhatsApp with country code picker
• YouTube, Facebook, Instagram links

**Customize**
• Basic QR colors and module shapes

**Export & share**
• Free JPEG and PNG
• High-quality transparent PNG (1000×1000) unlocked with a short rewarded ad
• Share or save to your gallery

**Scan**
• Camera scanner with copy, open, and share actions

**Privacy**
• Codes are generated and scanned on your device
• Camera is used only for scanning; frames are not uploaded by the app
• Ads via Google AdMob (consent where required)

Coming soon: editable vector SVG export.
