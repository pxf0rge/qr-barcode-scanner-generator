Privacy Policy

App: VectorQR — QR & Barcodes
Package: com.smartapps.vectorqr
Effective date: September 21, 2026
Last updated: September 21, 2026

Summary: VectorQR does not create user accounts and does not collect, sell, or upload your personal information to our own servers. Camera, photos, contacts, and scan history stay on your device (unless you choose to share or save them yourself). The app shows ads via Google AdMob, which is a third-party service that may process device-level advertising data under Google’s policies.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android application VectorQR — QR & Barcodes (“VectorQR”, “the App”), package name com.smartapps.vectorqr, published by SmartApps (“we”, “us”, or “developer”).

Contact for privacy questions: privacy.smartapps.vectorqr@gmail.com

2. Information we (the developer) collect

We do not collect personal data on our servers. VectorQR has no developer backend for analytics profiles, account registration, or cloud backups of your content.

Specifically, we do not:

  • Require sign-in or collect name, email, phone number, or payment details
  • Upload scanned QR/barcode contents to our servers
  • Upload camera frames, gallery images, contacts, or exported files to our servers
  • Sell or rent user data

Local preferences (for example theme or scan beep settings) and history may be stored only on your device using on-device storage (such as SharedPreferences). Uninstalling the App removes this local data.

3. Permissions and how they are used

3.1 Camera

The Camera permission is used solely to scan QR codes and barcodes in real time. Camera imagery is processed on-device for scanning and is not recorded or uploaded by VectorQR to our servers.

3.2 Photos / media / storage

Storage or photo/media access (as required by your Android version) may be used to:

  • Scan a QR/barcode from an image you pick from the gallery
  • Save generated codes to your gallery when you choose “Save”
  • Share exported images/files through the system share sheet when you choose “Share”

Files you save or share remain under your control on the device or in the destination you select. We do not upload those files to our servers.

3.3 Contacts (optional)

Contacts access may be requested only to help you import contact fields into a QR (vCard) that you generate. Contact data is processed on-device and is not uploaded to our servers.

3.4 Internet / network state

Internet access is used to load ads (Google AdMob), show consent forms where required (Google User Messaging Platform), and open links you tap (for example Privacy Policy or Rate).

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

VectorQR uses Google AdMob to show the following ad formats:

  • Banner ads
  • Interstitial ads
  • Rewarded ads

AdMob is provided by Google. To deliver, measure, and (where applicable) personalize ads, Google and its partners may collect or process device identifiers (such as advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device/app info, and interaction data, subject to your consent choices and applicable law (including GDPR/UMP consent messages where required).

We do not control Google’s independent processing. Learn more here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob & privacy
  • How Google uses information from apps

You can reset or limit your advertising ID in Android settings, and you may manage privacy options from within the App settings where Google’s consent/privacy form is available.

5. Children

VectorQR is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through third-party advertising services, contact us and we will help review the matter with applicable partners.

6. Data security

Because we do not operate a user-data backend for VectorQR content, the primary security boundary is your device. Keep your device updated and use Android permission controls to revoke Camera, Photos, or Contacts access at any time.

7. Data retention

We retain no personal user profiles on developer servers. On-device history and settings remain until you clear them in the App or uninstall VectorQR. Advertising data retention is governed by Google AdMob / Google policies.

8. Your choices

  • Deny or revoke Camera, Photos/Storage, or Contacts permissions in system settings
  • Clear local history from the App (where available)
  • Use Android advertising / privacy controls and in-app privacy options for ads consent
  • Uninstall the App to remove local VectorQR data

9. International users

If you are in the EEA/UK/Switzerland or other regions with consent requirements for ads, Google’s User Messaging Platform may present a consent form before personalized ads are requested.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect product or legal changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.
